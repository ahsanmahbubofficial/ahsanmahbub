The story

From a phone camera in Dhaka to brands that sell.

I'm Ahsan. I started making YouTube videos on my phone in 2019. While others chased fame, I learned editing, and it earned me my first online income.

In 2021 I joined a friend's production team as an intern and edited 50+ videos. A year later I started my own streetwear brand, Triple A. Instead of paying for ads, I made videos teaching people how the T-shirt business really works. That content grew to 30k+ YouTube subscribers and 50k+ Facebook followers in 2023, and sold 1,000+ hoodies and 1,500+ T-shirts the year after.

In 2025 I opened my first office in Dhaka as a full-service content studio. In 2026 I launched CholoBD, a community app, and grew it to 70k+ followers and 47k website users with zero ad spend. Along the way, I created and hosted Bangladesh Got Latent with some of the country's best-known creators, and trained 55 entrepreneurs to sell online.

Today I lead CholoBD. Every result I've had came from content, not ad budgets, and that's what I bring to your brand.

You're in Bangladesh. How do time zones work?+

Editing happens while you sleep, so videos are often waiting for you in the morning. For calls, I'm available during US mornings and UK afternoons.

Do I need a professional camera?+

No. A recent phone, a cheap lavalier mic and a window for light is enough. I'll send you a simple setup guide and shot list.

How do I send footage?+

Upload to a shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder. You'll get edits back in the same place, with a simple review link for feedback.

How do payments work?+

The pilot is paid upfront. For monthly plans, you pay 50% to start and 50% when your first batch is delivered. After that, plans are billed monthly in advance. Pay by card (Visa, Mastercard or Amex) or bank transfer, and cancel any time with 7 days' notice.
